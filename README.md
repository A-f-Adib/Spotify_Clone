<h2>Spotify_Clone 🎧</h2> 
The Ultimate Music Streaming Experience
<h3> Description:</h3>
The Spotify Clone is a feature-rich iOS app that brings the power of music streaming to your fingertips. With a sleek SwiftUI-powered interface, real-time song playback, and seamless playlist management, this app recreates the essence of a premium music platform.

<h3>🚀 Features</h3>
🎶 Music Streaming – Play songs on demand with smooth playback.<br>
📂 Playlist Management – Create, edit, and save your favorite playlists.<br>
🔍 Search & Discover – Find artists, albums, and trending tracks instantly.<br>
🎛 Dynamic UI – Stunning animations and transitions for a premium feel.<br>
🎚 Custom Music Player – Interactive controls with play, pause, skip, and seek options.<br>
🌙 Dark & Light Mode – Adaptive UI for a sleek look in any environment.<br>
